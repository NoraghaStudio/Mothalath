Website Design Plan for "Triangle Design for Engineering"
(مثلث الرسم للاستشارات الهندسية)

1. OVERALL AESTHETIC & THEME
---------------------------------------------------------
Color Palette:
- Primary Color: Deep Emerald Green (Based on the company profile and Vision 2030 branding on the cover).
- Secondary Color: Dark Grey/Charcoal (for text and structural accents).
- Background: Clean White and abstract light greys to give a spacious, architectural feel.
- Patterns: Subtle triangular geometric patterns and lines serving as a motif in the background across the site, playing off the company name "Triangle" (مثلث).

Typography:
- Arabic: Modern, highly legible corporate fonts like "Cairo", "Tajawal", or "Almarai".
- English: Sleek sans-serif fonts such as "Montserrat", "Roboto", or "Inter".

Visual Style:
- Professional, corporate, and engineering-focused.
- Uses high-quality photography matching the profile (e.g., modern buildings, surveying equipment, blueprints mixed with real-world transitions).
- Hover effects on cards that slice diagonally or highlight with the primary green color to match the triangle theme.


2. WEBSITE ARCHITECTURE (SITEMAP)
---------------------------------------------------------
The website should feature a sticky navigation bar at the top with a multi-language switcher (Arabic / English), considering the profile has bilingual elements.

A. Home Page (الصفحة الرئيسية)
B. About Us (من نحن)
C. Services (خدماتنا)
D. Our Experience & Portfolio (خبراتنا ومعرض الأعمال)
E. Partners (شركاؤنا)
F. Contact Us (اتصل بنا)


3. DETAILED PAGE BREAKDOWNS
---------------------------------------------------------

A. HOME PAGE
- Hero Section: A full-screen slider or high-quality background video showing modern engineering works (blueprints transitioning to buildings).
  * Headline: "دقة التصميم تبدأ من مثلث الرسم" (Design accuracy begins with Triangle of Drawing).
  * Sub-headline: Comprehensive Engineering Consulting & Project Management.
  * Call to Action (CTA): "Request a Consultation" or "Discover Our Services".
- Quick About Section: A short introduction focusing on their alignment with Vision 2030 (as seen in the profile).
- Services Overview (Grid Layout): 6 cards featuring their core offerings:
  1. Building Licenses (رخص بناء)
  2. Exterior & Interior Design (تصميم وواجهات)
  3. Supervision & Management (إشراف وإدارة مشروعات)
  4. Surveying (مساحة)
  5. Technical Office (أعمال المكتب الفني)
  6. Follow-up Works (متابعة الأعمال)
- Trust Badges: A banner showcasing their Commercial Registry (س.ت 1009160525) and "Certified Experience" badges (خبرة معتمدة).

B. ABOUT US PAGE
- Company Identity: A detailed block about how it started.
- Leadership Words (كلمة الإدارة و المدير العام): Two elegant text blocks alongside professional portraits or office imagery, conveying their commitment to quality.
- Vision, Mission & Goals (الرؤية والأهداف والرسالة): A 3-column section.
  * Vision: Icon of an eye.
  * Mission: Icon of a target/flag.
  * Goals: Icon of ascending graph or a blueprint.
- Organizational Structure (الهيكل التنظيمي): An interactive or beautifully styled tree chart showing:
  * General Manager -> Project Management, Business Dev, Financial Dept, HR, Surveying Dept.

C. SERVICES PAGE
A dedicated page that thoroughly explains each department:
- Surveying and Level Works (أعمال المساحة والمناسيب): Showcasing the use of total stations, soil study capabilities, and geographical site prep.
- Follow-up Works (متابعة الأعمال): Detailed explanation on how they supervise on-site execution, ensuring direct technical & administrative delivery.
- Technical Office (أعمال المكتب الفني): Mentioning quantity surveying, shop drawings, etc.
- Each service block should have an image on one side and bullet points on the other, alternating down the page.

D. OUR EXPERIENCE & PORTFOLIO
- A filterable gallery where users can sort by:
  * Residential
  * Commercial
  * Governmental
  * Interior/Exterior
- Each project card opens a modal or sub-page showing the project's specifics, total area, client, and before/after pictures.

E. PARTNERS PAGE
- A clean, grid layout displaying logos of their partners, clients, and authorized entities to build immediate trust.

F. CONTACT US PAGE
- Contact Form: Name, Phone, Email, Service Needed, and a text box for the message.
- Office Information Panel:
  * Address: طريق الأمير محمد بن سلمان – قرطبة الدور الأول مكتب رقم (6) (Prince Mohammed Bin Salman Road - Qurtubah, 1st Floor, Office 6).
  * Phone Numbers: 0549001760 / 0549007520.
  * CR Number: 1009160525.
- Embedded Google Map showing a pin with the company's logo.
- QR Code integration (as seen on the profile cover) that users can scan to save their contact info easily.


4. USER EXPERIENCE (UX) & INTERACTIVITY
---------------------------------------------------------
- Responsiveness: Fully optimized for mobile / tablets, as a lot of clients might browse via mobile.
- RTL / LTR Support: Perfect Right-to-Left alignment for the Arabic version, and standard LTR for the English version.
- Micro-interactions: Triangular wipe-transitions when moving between pages. Soft fade-ins for text and images when scrolling down to give it a premium, modern feel.
- WhatsApp Integration: A floating WhatsApp button in the corner for instant messaging, which is heavily utilized in Saudi Arabia.
